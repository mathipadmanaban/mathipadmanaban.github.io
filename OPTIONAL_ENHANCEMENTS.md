# Optional Website Enhancements

These are **optional** improvements if you want to go beyond the current professional standard. Your website is already excellent for academic applications.

## Quick Wins (30 mins - 2 hours)

### 1. Add Research Images
Add 1-2 key figures to your research projects:
- Overtaking detection system diagram
- Distance estimation visualization
- Sample results from your work

Place in `assets/images/research/` and embed in research.md

### 2. Add Custom Favicon
Replace default favicon with your initials or university logo
- Create 32x32 icon
- Place in assets/images/favicon.ico
- Add to _config.yml

### 3. Add Google Analytics
Track who visits your site (useful to know reach)
- Get Google Analytics ID
- Add to _config.yml

## Medium Effort (2-4 hours)

### 4. Create a Research Highlights Section
On homepage, add visual cards showing:
- Key publications with thumbnails
- Research software screenshots
- Awards/recognition icons

### 5. Add "News" or "Updates" Section
Recent accomplishments (accepted papers, awards, talks)
- Shows you're active and productive
- Easy to update

### 6. Enhanced About Section
Add a professional photo or research image to homepage
- Makes it more personal
- Creates connection with viewer

## Higher Effort (4-8 hours)

### 7. Interactive Research Demos
Embed videos or interactive visualizations:
- YouTube/Vimeo video of your system in action
- GIFs showing detection/tracking results
- Interactive plots of results

### 8. Blog/Research Posts
Short posts explaining your research to broader audience:
- Helps with science communication
- Shows ability to explain complex ideas
- Good for outreach and broader impacts

### 9. Collaboration/Contact Form
Make it easier for potential collaborators to reach out
- Simple contact form
- Clear call-to-action
- Email signup for updates

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't make it too flashy/gimmicky
❌ Don't prioritize design over content
❌ Don't add features that are hard to maintain
❌ Don't copy commercial website styles (keep academic tone)
❌ Don't add autoplay videos or animations
❌ Don't make navigation complicated

## Priority Ranking for Your Situation

**If you have limited time before applications:**
1. **Do nothing** - your site is already excellent
2. **If you have 1-2 hours**: Add research images to make it more visual
3. **If you have 4+ hours**: Add a news/updates section

**After you secure a position:**
- Add blog posts about your research
- Create video demos
- Build out more interactive elements

---

Remember: **Your research speaks for itself.** A clean, professional website that makes your work easy to find and understand is exactly what you need.
